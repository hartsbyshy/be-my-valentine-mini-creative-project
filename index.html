THE CAMPAIGN

Valentine's Day isn't only about romance. It's about the butterflies before a date, the people who feel like home, and the moments when you choose yourself.

Be My Valentine celebrates romantic love, friendship, and self-love through relatable experiences, expressive illustrations, and playful visual storytelling.

At the heart of the campaign is the idea of finding your muse. A muse is someone or something that inspires you, brings out your confidence, and encourages self-expression. It could be someone you love, the friends who make you feel your best, or simply yourself.

Through EyeMuse, the campaign encourages everyone to embrace what inspires them and express their individuality, because your muse can be anyone, including you.
